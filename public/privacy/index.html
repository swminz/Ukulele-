UkePocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

UkePocket is a mobile application for ukulele practice, including tuning, metronome, songs, music sheets, and chords.

UkePocket is designed as a local-first application. It does not require an account or sign-in, and UkePocket does not currently provide cloud storage for your music.

Information You Provide

UkePocket does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

You may create or import content such as:

  • Songs, lyrics and chords
  • PDF music sheets
  • Audio files associated with songs
  • Custom chords
  • App preferences and settings

This content is stored locally within the app on your device.

Microphone

UkePocket requests microphone access when you use the ukulele tuner.

The microphone allows UkePocket to listen to your instrument and detect pitch and frequency so that it can provide tuning feedback.

Tuner audio is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to UkePocket servers.

UkePocket does not use microphone access for advertising or user profiling.

Local Storage

Songs, PDFs, chords, audio files and settings are stored locally within the application's storage.

UkePocket does not currently require an online account or cloud music library.

If you use Backup & Restore, you can export your locally stored content to a backup file. You decide where that file is stored or shared.

How We Use Information

Information stored in UkePocket is used to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • Tuning your ukulele
  • Running the metronome
  • Managing songs and lyrics
  • Viewing music sheets
  • Managing chords
  • Remembering preferences
  • Creating and restoring backups

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

UkePocket does not sell or rent your personal information.

Your locally stored content remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

When you use your device's sharing functionality, such as sharing a backup file or the UkePocket app link, the information is handled by the service or application you choose to share it with.

Third-Party Services

The Android version of UkePocket is distributed through Google Play.

UkePocket does not currently use an account system, cloud database, advertising network, or third-party analytics service for user profiling.

The separate UkePocket web/PWA version may be hosted through Vercel. This web version is not required to use the Android application.

Third-party platform providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Microphone:
Used by the tuner to detect the pitch of your ukulele. Tuner audio is processed locally and is not uploaded to UkePocket servers.

Internet access:
Used where required by the application/platform for web-based resources and external links. UkePocket does not use Internet access to upload your locally stored music to a UkePocket cloud account.

Data Deletion

You can delete content using the application's available controls.

You can also clear the application's data or uninstall UkePocket through your device settings.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove songs, PDFs, audio files, settings and other locally stored content that has not been backed up.

Exported backup files are outside the application's storage and remain wherever you chose to save or share them.

Children's Privacy

UkePocket does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because UkePocket does not require account creation or intentionally collect personal information, it does not maintain personal profiles for users.

Security

UkePocket is designed to minimize information leaving your device.

You are responsible for protecting your device and any backup files you create.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if UkePocket's functionality or privacy practices change.

The latest version will include an updated "Last updated" date.
